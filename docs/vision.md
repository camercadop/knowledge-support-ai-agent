# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

Enterprise Knowledge AI Platform (EKAP) is a platform for building secure, production-ready AI knowledge assistants over enterprise data.

Its mission is to transform structured and unstructured information into accessible, explainable, and trustworthy knowledge using Large Language Models.

Unlike traditional chatbots, EKAP focuses on the entire enterprise AI lifecycle, including ingestion, retrieval, AI safety, analytics, evaluation, and operational excellence.
